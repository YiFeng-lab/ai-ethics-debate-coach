DESIGN GOAL

Reasoning practice through debate

THE PRODUCT QUESTION

How can an AI conversation
make room for better reasoning?

The design moves a learner from receiving information to stating a claim, defending it and reconsidering it.

01 / NEED

Active engagement.

Students need opportunities to work through AI ethics and governance questions. The coach is designed to keep their reasoning at the center of the interaction.

02 / BEHAVIOR

Challenge, then deepen.

Begin with a counterargument. Ask for justification before widening the discussion to ethical tensions and regulatory complexity.

03 / CLOSURE

A purposeful ending.

Use a four-to-five-round target to give the exchange a clear arc, then invite reflection instead of extending the disagreement indefinitely.

PROMPT & BEHAVIOR ITERATION

Rigorous.
Supportive.

The project’s design reflection describes early responses as too rigid or confrontational. Personality and rule changes aimed to keep the intellectual challenge while making the tone more encouraging.

KNOWLEDGE & LEARNING DESIGN

Ground the debate.
Keep the learner thinking.

The knowledge-base design brings together AI governance, fairness, bias, misinformation and ethics. Source materials use policy cases to support more concrete challenges.

Bozkurt’s generative AI literacy framing—Know What, Know How, Know Why—provides a conceptual reference for the learning experience.

Policy examples in the original materials are discussion contexts; their current status needs checking when preparing a learning activity.
